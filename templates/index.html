Mathematical Scoring Model

The overall match score is determined by a transparent linear combination of four normalized components:

Overall Match Score = (0.45 × Sskills) + (0.25 × Ssimilarity) + (0.20 × Sexperience) + (0.10 × Seducation)

1. Skills Match (45%)

Weight: 45%

Canonical taxonomy matching with synonym normalization (e.g. react.js → React, k8s → Kubernetes). Calculates exact overlap percentage between required competencies and candidate resumes.

2. Text Similarity (25%)

Weight: 25%

TF-IDF vector representation combining unigrams and bigrams with cosine similarity measurement. Measures contextual vocabulary alignment while penalizing irrelevant keyword stuffing.

3. Relevant Experience (20%)

Weight: 20%

Isolates professional work history, parsing employment dates and duration phrases. Computes ratio to target experience targets (e.g., 4+ years) and recognizes leadership seniority (Senior, Lead, Staff).

4. Education & Certs (10%)

Weight: 10%

Evaluates degree hierarchy (Ph.D., Master's, Bachelor's, Associate's) paired with recognized industry certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP, CKA, Scrum Master).

Ethical AI & Blind Merit Policy

Gender & Pronouns

Religion & Beliefs
